EMEC Shapinsay Sound
====================

EMEC Shapinsay Sound is a proposed scale tidal-test site in the waters between
mainland Orkney and Shapinsay.

**Development status:** The site has been assessed as a scale tidal-test
location. See the
`project record <https://tethys.pnnl.gov/project-sites/emec-shapinsay-sound-scale-tidal-test-site>`_.

**Why the site is useful:** A scale test site can support staged deployment of
multiple devices or a small array, giving developers a setting to test array
interactions, subsea electrical infrastructure, installation methods, and
environmental monitoring before commercial deployment. It is also useful for
validating resource and environmental models against focused field campaigns.

**Site challenges:** Development must balance energy-resource assessment,
environmental baseline characterisation, navigation, and the constraints of a
multi-user channel. Available site data are limited, making baseline surveys
and targeted measurements particularly important before deployment.
