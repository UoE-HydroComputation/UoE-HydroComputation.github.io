EMEC Fall of Warness
====================

EMEC's Fall of Warness tidal test site is west of Eday in Orkney and is one of
the world's principal tidal-energy test locations.

**Development status:** EMEC is pursuing consent for up to 50 MW of tidal
capacity. See the `Fall of Warness facility page <https://www.emec.org.uk/facilities/tidal-test-site/>`_.

**Why the site is useful:** The established test infrastructure makes Fall of
Warness particularly valuable for demonstrating turbines in real tidal flow,
testing installation and retrieval procedures, assessing reliability and
performance, and collecting environmental-monitoring evidence. Its planned
scale-up also provides a bridge between single-device testing and array-scale
development.

**Site challenges:** As an active multi-berth test site, development must
coordinate devices, subsea infrastructure, navigation, environmental
monitoring, and access within a high-energy flow environment.
