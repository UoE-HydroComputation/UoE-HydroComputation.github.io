Westray South
=============

Westray South is a tidal-lease area in Westray Firth, Orkney.

**Development status:** The site is under development by Orbital Marine Power.
See the
`Westray tidal-array project record <https://tethys.pnnl.gov/project-sites/orbital-marine-power-westray-tidal-array>`_.

**Why the site is useful:** Westray South is relevant to the transition from
demonstration turbines to a commercial array. It offers a case for assessing
array-scale energy yield, wake effects, export infrastructure, and the
interaction between technology choice and practical marine operations.

**Site challenges:** Progressing an array from development to operation
requires managing the energetic tidal resource alongside array layout,
grid connection, marine logistics, and environmental consenting.
