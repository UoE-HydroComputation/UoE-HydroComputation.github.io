Ness of Duncansby
=================

Ness of Duncansby lies at the eastern entrance to the Pentland Firth, close to
Stroma.

**Development status:** Lease options have been agreed at different times, but
there is no current development. See the
`Ness of Duncansby project record <https://tethys.pnnl.gov/project-sites/ness-duncansby-tidal-array>`_.

**Why the site is useful:** The site is a valuable test case for hydrodynamic
models and array layout studies in a complex headland flow. It allows the
effects of coherent, large-scale flow structures on resource estimates and
device loading to be examined before deployment.

**Site challenges:** Large-scale vortices shed from Stroma can pass through the
site, creating highly variable flow conditions for turbine loading and array
layout. Available site data are limited, increasing the importance of targeted
measurements for resource characterisation and model validation.
