EMEC Stronsay Firth
===================

Stronsay Firth is a proposed tidal-energy development area in eastern Orkney.

**Development status:** The site was announced by EMEC, but there is no active
development and it does not appear to remain under EMEC's control. See EMEC's
`development-site announcement <https://www.emec.org.uk/press-release-new-orkney-development-site/>`_.

**Why the site is useful:** The site remains a useful planning case for a
future Orkney array-development location. It highlights the work needed to
move from a site announcement to a deployable project: updated metocean and
resource surveys, environmental assessment, consenting, grid planning, and
developer commitment.

**Site challenges:** Any future development would need to re-establish site
control, consenting, and a current resource and environmental evidence base
before progressing to deployment.
