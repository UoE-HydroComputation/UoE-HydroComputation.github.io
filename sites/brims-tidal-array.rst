Brims Tidal Array
=================

The Brims Tidal Array, formerly known as Cantick Head, is a tidal-lease area
off south-west Orkney.

**Development status:** There is no current development at the site.

**Why the site is useful:** Brims is a useful feasibility case for combining
deep-water engineering constraints with detailed resource and array-layout
studies. Its former Cantick Head identity also makes it relevant when comparing
historic lease and development records with the current site definition.

**Site challenges:** Water depths of approximately 70--100 m present demanding
foundation, installation, and operations requirements. The site is also
affected by vortices generated off Swona, adding flow variability to turbine
and array design.
