Research
========

.. raw:: html

   <p class="page-intro">We use computational fluid dynamics, coastal-ocean models and data analysis to investigate the movement of water and its consequences for people, ecosystems and infrastructure.</p>

Our research sits at the intersection of environmental fluid mechanics and computational engineering. We develop methods, validate them against observations where possible, and apply them to questions where better understanding of flow can improve a decision.

Focus areas
-----------

.. raw:: html

   <div class="list-grid">
     <div><h3>Marine energy and offshore systems</h3><p>Resource assessment, array design, operational control and environmental effects of tidal and other marine-energy technologies.</p></div>
     <div><h3>Coastal, estuarine and fluvial processes</h3><p>Hydrodynamics, mixing, sediment and water-quality processes in changing natural systems.</p></div>
     <div><h3>Numerical methods and software</h3><p>Reliable, scalable and reproducible modelling tools for scientific and engineering applications.</p></div>
     <div><h3>Optimisation and uncertainty</h3><p>Efficient methods for comparing design options and communicating the confidence and limits of model results.</p></div>
   </div>

-----------------

The following projects illustrate the group’s work across digital twins, open modelling workflows and marine-energy decision support.
